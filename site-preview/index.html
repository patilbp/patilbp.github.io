01 / The work part

How I ended up doing this work.

I started out working with reporting and analytics. Over the years, I've moved closer to building the products themselves—talking to the people who need them, understanding what's missing, and figuring out how to make the tools work.

At Black Book, I built the BB Reporting Navigator, a self-service product for exploring report dependencies and changes. I'm also leading the architecture and technical research for a new in-house analytics platform. I enjoy being involved from the first conversations through the technical decisions and, eventually, delivery.

A working product is more interesting to me than a perfect presentation about one.
