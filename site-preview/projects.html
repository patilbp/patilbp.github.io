01 / A closer look
Valor Compounding Pharmacy · Customer operations

Measuring more than the number of prescriptions typed.

The pharmacy's customer-care team handled calls, tickets, schedules, and prescriptions. But performance was being judged largely by prescription volume. I built a system that looked at the different work people were actually doing.

5 data sources15+ KPIsRole-based analytics
Project on GitHub ↗
What wasn't working

One number didn't tell the whole story.

Prescription volume alone missed the time people spent on calls, service requests, schedules, and other work. That made it difficult to assess different roles against the work each person was actually responsible for.

The work

Five sources. More than fifteen KPIs.

I brought together calls, prescriptions, tickets, schedules, and employee data into a role-based performance analytics system. Different roles could be evaluated using targets that matched their responsibilities rather than one shared prescription count.

The outcome

Clearer measures for the team.

Employee performance metrics improved 30% across Q1–Q2 2023. The system also supported workforce planning and more transparent employee appraisals.

Another part of the job

Why were so many calls being missed?

I analyzed hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly call patterns and found a roughly 40% missed-call rate during peak hours. Extending Five9 history from three to twelve months helped guide staffing changes. Missed calls fell to 15% (a 62.5% relative reduction), and Zendesk tickets dropped from 120–150 to 40–50 a day (~67% reduction).
